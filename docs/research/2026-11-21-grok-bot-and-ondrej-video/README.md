# Grok Bot Pricing and the David Ondrej Video

Researched 2026-11-21.

## What Grok Bot costs (unconfirmed)

- OpenMausBot's README describes Grok Bot as "xAI's roster of bots on one shared cloud computer,
  Grok only, from a SuperGrok or Cursor plan."
- Reports conflict. One from October 2026 (pricingsaas.com) says xAI retired the SuperGrok line and
  now sells Free, Plus ($8 a month), Premium ($30), Super ($100) and Ultra ($200). Earlier 2026
  reports put SuperGrok at $30 a month. In August 2026 Grok Bot reportedly opened to all SuperGrok plans.
- **Open:** which current tier includes Grok Bot, and its price. Check xAI's own pricing page before
  the trial's cost comparison is written.

## David Ondrej's video this week

- The operator heard Ondrej covered OpenMausBot. He did not, as far as could be checked.

  (2026-11-15, 56:54) builds his own clone, **SuperBot**, in Rust with Claude Code, Cloudroom and
  Opus 5.5, and deploys it to a VPS. Sponsored by Hostinger and Cloudroom.
- Chapters are a build log (planning, frontend, backend, VPS deploy, branding, agent loops, final UI).
  No review or comparison chapter.
- [davidondrej/superbot](https://github.com/davidondrej/superbot): MIT, Rust, 397 stars, 25 commits,
  created and last pushed 2026-11-15. A one-sitting demo, not maintained software.
